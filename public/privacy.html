Privacy

Your details

Short and plain: what this site collects, why, and what you can ask for. Last updated 31 October 2026.

Who runs this site

anassaleh.me belongs to Anas Al Shikh Saleh, a visual artist and art director based in Dubai, UAE. For anything about your details, email hello@anassaleh.me.

What the contact form collects

Only what you type: your name, email, phone or WhatsApp number, company, the services you pick, budget, timing, how you found me, links and your brief. The time of the request and your country (from the network, not your exact location) are added.

Why

To reply to your request, discuss your project and send a quotation. Your details are never sold, shared for advertising or added to a mailing list.

Where it is kept

Each request arrives as an email in my inbox, is saved in my private Notion workspace, and a backup copy is stored with Cloudflare, which hosts this site. Only I have access.

How long

As long as the conversation or project needs it, and for my business records after that. You can ask me to delete it at any time.

Visits and cookies

This site sets no cookies. Visits are counted with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and does not track you across sites. To load pages faster, your browser keeps a copy of the site's content for the current tab only, and it is cleared when you close it.

Videos

Films play from Vimeo (with do not track on) or YouTube's privacy enhanced player. They only load when you press play, and from then on their own privacy policies apply.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the details you sent, or to stop being contacted. Email hello@anassaleh.me and I will handle it promptly.
